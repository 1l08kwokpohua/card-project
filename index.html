🔒 保密通知 Confidentiality Notice

📄 文件编号: CNF-2026-006
第一条 · 总则 Article 1 · General Principles
本通知所涉及的全部信息均属机密资料，受相关法律法规及内部保密制度保护。 所有接收本通知的人员（以下简称“接收方”）须严格遵守本通知所列各项规定。
All information covered by this notice constitutes confidential material and is protected by applicable laws, regulations, and internal confidentiality policies. Every recipient of this notice (hereinafter “Recipient”) shall strictly comply with all provisions set forth herein.
本通知适用于任何以口头、书面、电子或其他形式披露的信息，无论其载体为何。
This notice applies to any information disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or in any other form, regardless of the medium.
第二条 · 保密义务 Article 2 · Confidentiality Obligations
接收方同意并承诺：在任何情况下，未经授权不得以任何方式持有、复制、转发、 披露、传播或使用本通知所涉信息。
The Recipient agrees and undertakes that, under no circumstances, without prior authorization, they shall not possess, copy, forward, disclose, disseminate, or use the information covered by this notice in any manner whatsoever.
接收方仅可在获得明确书面许可的前提下，在授权范围内处理相关信息， 且处理完毕后应立即归还或销毁所有相关资料（包括电子副本）。
The Recipient may only handle the relevant information within the authorized scope upon obtaining explicit written permission. Upon completion, all related materials (including electronic copies) shall be immediately returned or destroyed.
接收方不得将本通知的存在或内容向任何第三方透露，除非法律法规另有强制性规定。
The Recipient shall not disclose the existence or content of this notice to any third party, unless otherwise mandatorily required by applicable laws or regulations.
第三条 · 禁止行为 Article 3 · Prohibited Acts
⚠ 未经授权，任何个人或实体均不得持有或转发本通知所涉信息。
⚠ Without prior authorization, no individual or entity may possess or forward the information covered by this notice.
禁止行为包括但不限于：
（一）将信息存储于未经授权的设备或云端；
（二）通过电子邮件、即时通讯、社交媒体或任何网络渠道转发信息；
（三）在公开场合讨论、展示或泄露信息内容；
（四）对信息进行翻译、改编、逆向工程或任何形式的二次处理。
Prohibited acts include, but are not limited to:
(a) Storing the information on unauthorized devices or cloud services;
(b) Forwarding the information via email, instant messaging, social media, or any network channel;
(c) Discussing, displaying, or disclosing the content of the information in public;
(d) Translating, adapting, reverse-engineering, or otherwise reprocessing the information in any form.
第四条 · 法律责任 Article 4 · Legal Liability
任何违反本通知规定的行为，均被视为严重违反保密制度。 违规者将承担相应的民事、行政及/或刑事责任，包括但不限于赔偿损失、 支付违约金、被追究商业秘密侵权或国家秘密泄露等法律责任。
Any violation of the provisions of this notice shall be deemed a serious breach of the confidentiality system. The violator shall bear corresponding civil, administrative, and/or criminal liabilities, including but not limited to compensation for damages, payment of liquidated damages, and legal liability for trade secret infringement or state secret disclosure.
如因接收方违约导致信息泄露，接收方应承担由此产生的一切直接及间接损失， 并配合有关部门进行调查。
If information is disclosed due to the Recipient’s breach, the Recipient shall bear all direct and indirect losses arising therefrom and shall cooperate with the competent authorities in any investigation.
第五条 · 非指定收件人 Article 5 · Non-Intended Recipients
如您并非本通知的指定收件人，您特此被通知：
任何未经授权阅读、持有、复制或转发本通知内容的行为均属严格禁止。 请您立即删除所有相关记录（包括电子邮件、附件及任何副本）， 并以书面或电子邮件方式通知发件人。
If you are not the intended recipient of this notice, you are hereby notified that:
Any unauthorized reading, possession, copying, or forwarding of the content of this notice is strictly prohibited. Please immediately delete all related records (including emails, attachments, and any copies) and notify the sender in writing or by email.
第六条 · 保密期限 Article 6 · Term of Confidentiality
本通知所涉信息的保密义务自接收方首次接触该信息之日起生效， 并持续有效，直至该信息依法定程序被正式解密或公开， 或由信息权利方以书面形式明确解除保密义务为止。
The confidentiality obligations regarding the information covered by this notice shall take effect from the date on which the Recipient first accesses such information and shall remain in full force and effect until the information is officially declassified or made public in accordance with legal procedures, or until the information rights holder explicitly releases the confidentiality obligations in writing.
第七条 · 管辖与解释 Article 7 · Governing Law & Interpretation
本通知的解释、效力及执行均适用中华人民共和国法律。 因本通知引起的或与本通知有关的任何争议，应提交有管辖权的人民法院解决。
The interpretation, validity, and enforcement of this notice shall be governed by the laws of the People’s Republic of China. Any dispute arising from or in connection with this notice shall be submitted to the competent People’s Court for resolution.
本通知以中英文两种语言书写。如中英文版本存在任何歧义或冲突， 以中文版本为准（除非另有书面约定）。
This notice is written in both Chinese and English. In the event of any ambiguity or conflict between the Chinese and English versions, the Chinese version shall prevail (unless otherwise agreed in writing).
第八条 · 确认与接受 Article 8 · Acknowledgment & Acceptance
接收方一经接触本通知所涉信息，即视为已阅读、理解并同意接受 本通知全部条款的约束。如接收方不同意本通知任何条款， 应立即停止接触相关信息并通知发件方。
By accessing the information covered by this notice, the Recipient shall be deemed to have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by all terms of this notice. If the Recipient does not agree to any term of this notice, they shall immediately cease accessing the relevant information and notify the sender.
